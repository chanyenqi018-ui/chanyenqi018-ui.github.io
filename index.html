Featured projects

Your Light

An iPhone app that helps people take one small next step, kindly, when life feels heavy. I am the product manager. Version 1.0 is live on the App Store.

  • Problem: big tasks feel too heavy to start, and most apps add pressure with streaks and scores.
  • What I did: product vision, scope, UX and brand direction, content, pricing, and App Store release.
  • Business model: free to download now. A yearly plan with a 7-day free trial is planned for the next release.
  • Release: v1.0 live in English, Bahasa Melayu and Simplified Chinese. v1.1 is in preparation.

Mindous

A voice app where three minutes of your real voice tells people what you do for a living. No feed deciding what you hear. I am the product manager. Version 1.0 is live on the App Store.

  • Problem: text profiles are flat, and ranking feeds decide who gets heard.
  • What I did: positioning, product rules (time-ordered feed, private by default), safety features, brand and App Store release.
